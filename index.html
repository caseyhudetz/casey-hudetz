Casey Hudetz

Designer. Speaker. Filmmaker.

Senior Manager, Product Design at DocuSign · Chicago

Casey Hudetz is a designer, speaker, teacher, and award-winning filmmaker. He leads AI and design strategy at DocuSign, exploring how emerging technologies shape human experience. His work spans product design, documentary filmmaking, and international speaking on AI, speculative design, and creative technology.
